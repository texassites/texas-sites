The band

We Are Nightwire

Nightwire started as three friends and a broken tape machine in a Reykjavík basement. Six years, two continents and one very patient sound engineer later, we make alt-electronic music built for the last hour of daylight — droning synths, live drums, and a guitar that never quite stays in tune on purpose.

For Sundown Static we moved the whole rig into an abandoned grain silo, mic'd the walls, and hit record. What you hear is the room as much as the band.

Sóley NóttVocals / Synths
Bó EllisonGuitar / Tape
Mira KovačBass / Modular
