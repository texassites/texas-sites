DESIGN CONCEPT
  "Sundown Static" — the site of a fictional alt-electronic band, Nightwire.
  The look is a dark concert stage: a plum-black ink washed with warm coral
  and amber stage lighting rather than a single neon pop, with a mauve
  neutral for meta text. Poster-condensed display type sits over a humanist
  grotesque, and a mono voice carries catalog numbers and track durations
  like liner notes. Spinning vinyl, a city marquee and an interactive
  tracklist give it the feel of a record you can actually play.

  PALETTE
    #14101D  ink            (plum-black base, violet-biased neutral)
    #1C1628  raised surface
    #F3EEF7  paper          (cool off-white text)
    #9D8FB3  mauve          (meta / muted text)
    #FF6A5C  coral          (primary — warm stage light)
    #FFC24B  amber          (secondary — tickets, highlights)

  FONTS
    Display : Anton           (gig-poster condensed headlines)
    Body    : Hanken Grotesk  (humanist grotesque, UI + prose)
    Mono    : DM Mono         (track times, catalog numbers, labels)

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html            The template (single long-scroll page)
  css/vendor/           Bootstrap 5 (minified, self-hosted)
  css/style.css         All custom design styles
  js/vendor/            Bootstrap 5 bundle (Popper included)
  js/main.js            Template behavior — vanilla JS, no jQuery
  img/                  Demo images (loremflickr / picsum)

NOTES
  - Bootstrap 5, no jQuery, no plugin or CDN dependencies. All icons are
    inline SVG, so nothing is blocked on strict-CSP hosts.
  - The tracklist, video and cart are front-end demos; wire them to your
    own audio embeds / store to go live.
  - The newsletter form validates on the front end — point <form action>
    at your provider.
  - Scroll reveals, the vinyl spin and marquee respect
    prefers-reduced-motion.
